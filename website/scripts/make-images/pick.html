Crop rectangle picker

— drag on the capture, then copy the rectangle into pictures.yaml

Drag on the capture to draw a rectangle. Drag its edges or its corners to resize it, and its middle to move it. The readout is the rectangle in the pixels of the capture, shadow included, which is what make-images.py cuts by. A rectangle thus holds only for captures taken the same way. If the window size or the scale of the screen changes, draw it again.
